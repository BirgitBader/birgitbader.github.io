Business wants outcomes, engineers want clarity, users want something that works, and systems need structure.
As a diplomat in tech, I make sure all of them are heard and that they come together.

Hi, I'm Birgit.
I find out where work gets stuck and why, then create what removes the friction: standards people actually follow, patterns teams can reuse, and a better developer experience.
Right now, I'm a Technical Writing Expert at OTTO in Hamburg, where I established technical writing as a discipline, made it an official role, and paved the way for more technical writers.

**Knowledge becomes valuable when it reaches the people and systems that need it, when and how they need it.**

## What I've built

- **A discipline at OTTO:** established the technical writer role, built a team, and created a documentation ecosystem that works across engineering teams.
- **An API Portal at OTTO:** owned vision and roadmap as product owner, grounded in user research with developers.
- **API guidelines and an API community:** introduced standards together with the teams who use them, not for them.
- **A Developer Portal at ePages:** hired to write API docs, ended up building the portal and the developer experience around it.
- **Knowledge for humans and AI:** structures that make implicit knowledge explicit, so AI tools have something reliable to work with.

## How I got here

I studied International Technical Communication at the University of Hildesheim and started out documenting wind turbines, consumer products, and industrial systems.
In 2015, I found my professional home in software development.
Along the way, I discovered that what fascinates me most isn't documentation itself.
It's helping knowledge move.
Twice, I was hired to write and ended up pioneering: [the full story is on my blog](/blog/hired-to-write-ended-up-pioneering/).

## Beyond the day job

I speak at conferences, mentor young talent, cultivate my website, and volunteer as a job ambassador at [Mein Jobbotschafter](https://meinjobbotschafter.de/en).

Curiosity brought me to this profession.
Sharing what I've learned is what keeps me excited about it.
